# Out of Scope: Internationalization (RTL, Localization)

Internationalization features such as RTL layout support and localization hooks are out of scope for the current roadmap.

## Rationale

The primary target markets and workloads for the initial release are LTR, English-primary. RTL layout and localization infrastructure add significant complexity to layout, text rendering, and string management that would delay the pre-GA release without proportionate adoption benefit for the initial user base.

## Future Consideration

This may be revisited after v1.0 GA based on community demand and contributor availability.
